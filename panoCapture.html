Stellarium panorama — open-data capture

This page renders a 3D globe locally in a hidden Cesium viewer using only open data (Esri World Imagery + AWS Terrain Tiles), sweeps a virtual camera through 37 directions, and stitches the captures into an equirectangular PNG suitable for Stellarium's maptex.

What you get: accurate satellite imagery draped on global terrain (SRTM-based), so distant mountain silhouettes appear correctly on the horizon. What you don't get: buildings — this data source has no 3D structures. Best suited to rural / landscape observing sites. Attribution to Esri / AWS terrain providers is required for non-personal use.
